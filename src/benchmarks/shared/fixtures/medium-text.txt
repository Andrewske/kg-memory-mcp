## Personal Reflections

Emma and I had our first real argument last night. She's upset that I've been working late almost every night this week, and honestly, she's right. I promised we'd have more quality time together, but I keep breaking that promise. The stress from work is bleeding into our relationship, and I hate it.

But I understand why she's questioning it when I'm constantly choosing work over spending time with her. We're supposed to move in together next month, and now I'm worried about whether we're ready for that step.

I've been thinking a lot about what my therapist Dr. Matthews said about work-life boundaries. He's right that I use work as an escape when I'm anxious about other things. Maybe I'm avoiding dealing with my commitment fears by throwing myself into projects.

Overall feeling: stressed, guilty, and overwhelmed, but trying to stay positive. Tomorrow is a new day.
